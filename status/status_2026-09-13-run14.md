  ran the full suite under `xvfb-run` before any change: **100 passed, 4

- Went further than prior runs' plain re-run: ran the real-display suite
  under `pytest-cov` (as Runs 12/13 did) repeatedly rather than once.
  **Found a genuine intermittent failure**:
  `tests/test_gui_integration.py::test_export_cancelled_dialog_does_not_write_a_file`
  failed in 2 of 3 runs under coverage instrumentation with
  `AssertionError: Timed out waiting for background task to complete.`,
  accompanied by a `PytestUnhandledThreadExceptionWarning`
  ("main thread is not in main loop") surfacing on a later, unrelated
  test.
- Root-caused rather than dismissing it as a fluke (section 36 policy —
  reproduce, isolate, fix the underlying issue):
  - `pytest-cov`'s line tracing over every `core`/`storage` call
    measurably slows the background worker thread that
    `gui/main_window.py._start_background_task` starts for baseline
    creation and scanning.
  - `tests/test_gui_integration.py`'s `_pump_until` test helper drove the
    real Tk event loop with a fixed **5-second** wall-clock timeout for
    that thread to finish.
  - When the (correctly slower, under tracing) worker thread didn't finish
    within 5s, `_pump_until` called `window.quit()` and gave up — but the
    worker thread was often still about to call `self.after(...)` to
    report completion. That call then raced against the now-stopped
    mainloop, raising "main thread is not in main loop" on a daemon
    thread, which pytest reports against whatever test happens to be
    running when the thread's exception surfaces (hence the misleading
    attribution to an unrelated test).
  - This is a **test-harness timing issue only**. `_start_background_task`
    in `gui/main_window.py` is unchanged production code, used exactly as
    the shipped app uses it (`app.py` calls `window.mainloop()` the same
    way).
- Fix: raised `_pump_until`'s default timeout from 5.0s to 20.0s in
  `tests/test_gui_integration.py`, with a comment explaining why (leaves
  headroom under coverage instrumentation without masking a genuine
  hang). No production code changed.
- Verified the fix: **5 consecutive** full real-display `pytest-cov` runs,
  all clean — **100 passed, 4 skipped**, **100% overall line coverage**
  preserved (previously ~2/3 runs failed on this test). Also re-ran a
  plain real-display suite (**100 passed, 4 skipped**) and the headless
  suite (**63 passed, 31 skipped**) without coverage — both still clean.
- Cross-checked `PROJECT_SPEC.md` and `SentinelLite.md` — kept
  byte-identical; updated the Development Progress paragraph to record
  this run.
